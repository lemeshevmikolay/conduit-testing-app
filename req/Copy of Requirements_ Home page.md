# Home page

1. Start page of the product. Users can get on the page from other pages:  
   1. by clicking the "Conduit" logo in the upper left corner of the page;  
   2. by clicking the "Home" header on the top of the page.

2. There is a banner with the words "Conduit A place to share your knowledge." on the green background.  
     
3. All the article content is placed in the Feed section. The feed section contains two tabs: "Your feed" (only for logged-in users) and "Global feed".  
     
4. The "Your feed" tab contains only the articles of the users the user is following. Empty by default.  
     
5. The "Global feed" tab contains the articles of all users. The articles are sorted from the newest to the oldest.  
     
6. There is pagination under the list of articles.  
     
7. The pagination contains pages from 1 to 50\. Only 10 articles per page are shown.  
     
8. Each article contains the following elements:  
   1. The avatar and the username of the author;  
   2. The date of creation in the DD.MM.YYYY format;  
   3. The article's title;  
   4. The article's description;  
   5. The "Read more..." link;  
   6. The "Like" button.

9. A user is redirected to the **User page** after clicking the username of the author of the article.  
     
10. A user is redirected to the [**Article page**](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1yr_SlQU3JBbYc4Vuff8fPMbikE0WAp3ShcC-GJfD8sg/edit#) after clicking the title, the description, or the "Read more..." link.

11. Only logged-in users can see the number of likes and can like an article.  
      
12. A user can like the article by clicking the "Like" button on the right of the article section.  
      
13. A user can unlike the article liked by him before by clicking the "Like" button on the right of the article section.

14. The "Popular tags" section should be placed on the right of the Feed section.  
      
15. By clicking a certain tag from the "Popular tags" section a user gets articles that contain this tag.
